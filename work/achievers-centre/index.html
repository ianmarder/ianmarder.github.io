The Playbook

For the 2026 Revenue Kickoff, we transformed a standard corporate meeting into a live "SportsCenter" broadcast. I architected the entire visual system — from the digital "desk" segments to physical assets — serving as the lead designer, showrunner, and even the "announcer" to keep the energy high.

The Vibe

High-Energy, Comedic, Budget Broadcast

Key Deliverables

Motion direction, 300+ custom slides, print assets, voiceover and pace-check

Warm up and stretches

Before a single slide was built, the visual direction needed to be locked. Proposal concepts established the tone, colour system, and motion language for the entire event.

Broadcast Package: Intro & Pause Screens

The event relied on "the flip" — switching seamlessly between the news desk and remote reporters. I designed motion graphics loops and high-energy pause screens to maintain the immersion during transitions.

Identifying our favourite sports

Well before the event, we sent out a questionnaire asking about attendance, timing, accomodation needs, and so on. Included in this questionnaire was the simple question "What's your favourite sport?". The answers to this question helped us build out a unique set of ID badges for the event, exclusively using our custom brand shapes. With 200+ attendees each receiving a custom card, the system had to be both distinctive and scalable. Getting to design a few one-offs was the absolute best part. Shoutout to Andrew Chambers and Emily Pearce for their extremely specific answers.

Building more than a presentation

I built and art-directed the entire slide ecosystem for every department. This involved custom template architecture that allowed speakers to deliver complex data while staying inside the "AchieversCenter" broadcast aesthetic.

Announcing...

To help elevate the parody, I provided the announcer voiceover and comedic direction for the segments, bridging the gap between high-level corporate messaging and engaging entertainment.

Reception & Reviews

Though it's hard to know whether a corporate event really took off or not, the feedback was genuinely positive. Attendees voiced particular appreciation for the consistency of the theme across presentations, well-paced timing and conversations, and a particular love for the unique ID badges.
