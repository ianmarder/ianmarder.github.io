Rebrand, Web & Sales Collateral • 2019–2021

QuicksortRX

A pharmacy analytics startup changed its name. I built the brand that came with it, then kept building what it needed to sell.

The Brief

AscendRx was pharmacy supply chain software built at the Medical University of South Carolina. I'd already designed its first brochure, case study, and website under that name. When it became QuicksortRX, the goal was simple: re-brand the business to increase visibility and earn trust in an industry that doesn't hand it out easily. Consistency, simplicity, and transparency became the three values everything had to answer to.

Role

Freelance Designer / Brand Lead

Industry

Healthcare / Pharmacy SaaS

Services

Discovery, Logo Design, Brand Guidelines, Web Design, Print Collateral, Sales Enablement

Same pitch, new face

The product didn't change overnight, so the story didn't either. What changed was how it looked while telling it. Here's the 2019 AscendRx brochure next to its 2020 QuicksortRX replacement.

Three routes to a Q

Two discovery sessions came first: one on the business, one on the people it was built for, the pharmacy buyers stuck in spreadsheets every day. That fed three distinct logo directions, each with its own flavour, all shown in the places a logo actually has to live.

The one that stuck

The third route won and went through a round of refinements: a clean wordmark, a superscript Rx, and a Q with a green tag that works on its own as an icon, favicon, or app tile.

Writing it down

An 18-page visual identity guide made the brand hand-off-able: logo rules, Montserrat over Open Sans, a deep-sea and tropics palette with red saved for calls to action, and a wave element that stands in for the pharmacy market's constant churn.

The package

Alongside the guide came the full logo suite, a colour library, business cards, letterhead, one-pager templates, slides, and LinkedIn banners. Existing material moved over too; the MUSC case study below is the same story, rebuilt in the new system.

Built to sell

A brand for a startup has one job: help close deals. The 2020 brochure leads with the buyer's problem, backs it with customer quotes, and ends on a guarantee.

And it kept going

The work carried on well past launch: homepage mockups, a 2021 brochure built around a new ASHP partnership, a sales proposal template, a technical paper, letterheads, and HTML email signatures.
