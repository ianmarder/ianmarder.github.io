The Studio

A simple collection of the various things I've physically made over the years. Recent focus on cartography, and astronomical endeavours.

Maps

I obviously loved the classic Lord of the Rings maps as a kid, but it was actually old pirate stories and the history of Oak Island which really led me to appreciate the detail and design of both fictional and genuine cartography.

What watercolour is the sky?

I've been obsessed with watercolour planets lately. I make a grid of circles on a piece of watercolour paper and then just... vibe. Once they're done, I cut them out and use them for all kinds of stuff, like little space squares and even cards with fun quotes.

The Simpsons collection

An enormous component of my humour and love of cartooning. I've been doodling them on sticky notes for like a decade. Occassionally I like to put them on black paper to make my mnml smpsn dreams come true. I think the mnml Abe is probably my favourite.
